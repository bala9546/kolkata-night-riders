1. How The System Works (In 3 Simple Steps)

Zero manual calculations. Zero awkward money chats.

1

Log the Bill

Whenever anyone pays for an Arsalan Biryani, Yellow Cab, Hotel, or Kulhad Chai, tap "+ Add Expense". Select who paid and check who shared the item.

2

Algorithm Calculates Fair Share

The app automatically runs a greedy debt simplification cash-flow algorithm. Instead of everyone paying everyone back in 15 different transfers, it condenses all debts into the absolute minimum number of direct transactions.

3

Scan & Pay Instantly via UPI

Head to the "Settlements" tab. Tap "Pay via UPI" to open GPay / PhonePe directly on mobile, or scan the dynamic QR code from another screen!

4. Settling Debts, Copying UPI ID & QR Downloads

Fast, safe transfers with zero UPI risk policy blocks.

A

1-Click Copy Payee UPI ID (Bypasses Bank Risk Policy)

To avoid strict bank app web risk policies, simply tap the "Copy UPI ID" chip. Open Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm, paste the copied ID, and transfer directly with 100% success!

B

Editable Amount (Partial Payments Allowed!)

You can edit the amount directly inside the settlement modal! If you owe ₹1,500 but only want to clear ₹500 today, type 500. The QR code dynamically regenerates, and when you mark it paid, your remaining debt automatically drops to ₹1,000.

C

Save / Download Dynamic QR Code

Tap "Save / Download QR". The app generates an image with the exact payee and amount. In Google Pay or PhonePe, tap the Scanner icon ➔ select "Upload from Gallery" ➔ pick the QR image to pay without typing!

D

Two-Way Anti-Abuse Handshake (Send Claim ➔ Receiver Confirms)

To prevent members from falsely clicking "Settled" without transferring money, our app uses a Two-Way Settlement Handshake:

  • Payer Action: Tap "I Have Paid (Send Verification Claim)". This alerts the receiver.
  • Receiver Verification: The receiver opens their bank/UPI app, checks that the amount entered their account, and clicks "✓ Confirm Money Received".
  • Protection: Debts are ONLY cleared once the receiver confirms! Debtor cannot falsely settle their own debt.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for squad members.

Is all our trip data saved permanently?
Yes! The web application is connected directly to a high-speed MongoDB Atlas Cloud database. Whenever you add or edit a bill, it is instantly written to the cloud cluster so nobody loses their data even if they close their browser.
How do I switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode?
Click the 🌙 / ☀️ icon in the top right corner of the navigation bar. Your preference is saved on your device.
Can we add more than 6 members?
Yes! Open "Squad Members" and type any new member's name and UPI ID in the top field. The app will generate a custom color avatar for them and recalculate all balances dynamically.
What happens if someone accidentally types a wrong bill amount?
Open the "All Expenses" tab, find the bill, and tap the trash 🗑️ icon to remove it. Then simply log the correct amount.
